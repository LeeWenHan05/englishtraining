Scenario B · 7 Cases

For every case: Scenario Core → Universal Block → Conclusion. Follow-up only if the lecturer asks.

Universal Block · memorise once

Before making a final decision, I would make sure the facts are clear and the situation is handled through the proper process.
I would keep the communication professional and explain the next step clearly.
I would also make sure the solution is fair to the people involved.
If the first step did not solve the problem, I would take the next proper step or ask the appropriate person for further support.
After that, I would review the situation to make sure the solution is working.
The goal is to solve the problem fairly and prevent the same issue from happening again.
I would also keep a simple record of the key facts, the action taken and the reason for the decision.
This makes the process easier to review and helps the organisation handle similar situations more consistently in the future.
